I submit the manuscript "Explanation Context and Bug-Repair Calibration in Open-Weight Code LLMs on
Novice Python Tasks" for consideration as a [article type].

The study asks whether an explanation placed in the prompt - accurate or misleading - changes how
well open-weight language models repair novice-level Python bugs and how confident they report
being. It contributes (1) a benchmark of 40 seeded bugs whose tests were validated with seeded
faults, alternative solutions and generated mutants; (2) a controlled four-condition comparison over
1,440 runs of three local models; and (3) a calibration analysis. The main findings are a null
result for the hypothesised harm of misleading explanations, severe over-confidence of a 1.5B model
that returned buggy code unchanged in almost half of its runs, and the observation that only 12 of
40 model-written explanations of buggy code were fully accurate.
